qualified legal counsel should be consulted before it is used with real parties or real
funds. Fields shown in square brackets must be completed for each individual deal before
that deal's version of the Agreement is sent to the parties. Sections marked as an open

approval within the Blockmediary platform.

\section*{Parties}
%---------------------------------------------------------------

This Trade Escrow Agreement is entered into between the Buyer, the Seller, and
Blockmediary. The Buyer is identified in Schedule 1 by full legal name, registered address
or country of residence, and wallet address. The Seller is identified in Schedule 1 on the
same basis. Blockmediary is identified by its legal entity name and registered address,
also recorded in Schedule 1. The Buyer and Seller are together referred to as the Parties.
Blockmediary acts as the escrow operator under this Agreement and is not a principal to
the underlying trade between the Buyer and the Seller.

\section*{Recitals}
%---------------------------------------------------------------

\begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.]
\item The Buyer and Seller have agreed to a commercial transaction for the sale and
purchase of goods described in Schedule 1, referred to as the Trade, on terms recorded in
their underlying sale contract.
\item The Parties wish to use the Blockmediary platform to manage payment for the Trade
through a smart contract documentary escrow, under which the Buyer's payment is held on
chain and released to the Seller upon verified presentation of compliant trade documents.
\item This Agreement sets out the terms on which Blockmediary operates the escrow, the
document release rules, and the rights of the Parties in the event of discrepancy,
objection, or dispute.
\item This Agreement is separate from, and does not replace, the underlying sale contract
between the Parties. Blockmediary is not a party to that contract and has no liability for
the underlying trade.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{1. Definitions}
%---------------------------------------------------------------

In this Agreement, the following defined terms apply. The Audit Ledger means
Blockmediary's append only off chain record of every state transition, reviewer decision,
and on chain transaction relating to this Deal. The Blockchain means the specific
Base network deployment, testnet or mainnet as specified in Schedule 1, on which the
Escrow Contract is deployed. A Compliant Presentation means a presentation of Required
Documents that satisfies all Release Rules specified in Schedule 1, as determined by
Blockmediary's document verification process. The Deal means the specific escrow
transaction governed by this Agreement, identified by the Deal ID recorded in Schedule 1.
A Discrepancy means a deficiency in a presented document that prevents a Compliant
Presentation, as particularised in a Discrepancy Notice. A Discrepancy Notice means a
written notice from Blockmediary to the Seller identifying each specific Discrepancy in a
submitted document set. The Escrow Amount means the amount in the Settlement Token, and
its fiat equivalent at the agreed rate, held by the Escrow Contract, as specified in
Schedule 1. The Escrow Contract means the smart contract deployed at the address specified
in Schedule 1 on the Blockchain that holds the Escrow Amount and enforces state
transitions. The Escrow Specification means the canonical structured document generated at
deal setup, identified by the hash recorded in Schedule 1, that is authoritative for the
Release Rules and the required document types; this Agreement incorporates the Escrow
Specification by reference. A Notice of Release means Blockmediary's written notification
to the Parties that a Compliant Presentation has been determined and that the Escrow
Amount will be released subject to the Objection Window. The Objection Window means the
period specified in Schedule 1, defaulting to forty eight hours, following a Notice of
Release, during which the Buyer may raise a valid Permitted Objection. A Permitted
Objection means an objection by the Buyer during the Objection Window on one or more of
the grounds specified in clause 7.2. The Release Rules mean the document compliance
conditions specified in the Escrow Specification and Schedule 1 that must be satisfied for
the Escrow Amount to be released to the Seller. The Required Documents mean the trade
documents specified in Schedule 1 that the Seller must present to trigger a compliance
determination. The Settlement Token means the stablecoin specified in Schedule 1 in which
the Escrow Amount is denominated and released. The Shipment Deadline means the date
specified in Schedule 1, being the latest date by which the Required Documents must
evidence shipment. The Trade Escrow Agreement, or the Agreement, means this document
including all Schedules.

\section*{2. The Escrow Arrangement}
%---------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection*{2.1 Nature of the Escrow}

Blockmediary operates a documentary escrow. The Buyer deposits the Escrow Amount on
chain, Blockmediary verifies whether the Seller's presented documents satisfy the Release
Rules, and the Escrow Amount is then released to the Seller upon a Compliant Presentation,
subject to the Objection Window, or returned to the Buyer on a Refund Condition.

\subsection*{2.2 Blockmediary's Role}

Blockmediary acts as an independent escrow operator and document verification service. It
is not a bank, payment institution, or e money institution; it is not a party to the
Parties' underlying sale contract; it is not a guarantor of the quality, condition, or
title of the goods; and it is not an issuing bank or confirming bank within the meaning of
UCP 600 \citep{ucp600}. Blockmediary's verification is based on the face of the documents
presented. It does not inspect goods, verify physical delivery, or investigate whether the
Seller's representations in the documents are true.

\subsection*{2.3 UCP 600 Inspired Release Logic}

Blockmediary's Release Rules are inspired by the International Chamber of Commerce's
Uniform Customs and Practice for Documentary Credits, UCP 600, but this Agreement is not a
letter of credit and Blockmediary is not an issuing bank \citep{ucp600}. Where this
Agreement is silent, UCP 600 principles provide interpretive guidance but are not binding.
The Escrow Specification remains authoritative.

\subsection*{2.4 Electronic Document Acceptance, Minimum Viable Product Scope}

For this Deal, the Seller must present scanned originals of the Required Documents, being
high resolution portable document format images of physically signed originals.
Electronic transferable records, including electronic bills of lading, are not accepted
under this Agreement unless Schedule 1 expressly specifies an Electronic Trade Documents
Act qualifying electronic system. \textit{Open discussion point: post minimum viable
product, add an electronic bill of lading pathway with the corresponding statutory
representation clause.}

\section*{4. Funding}
%---------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection*{4.1 Buyer Deposit}

The Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount in the Settlement Token to the Escrow Contract
address specified in Schedule 1 on the Blockchain on or before the Funding Deadline. The
deposit is made by the Buyer's wallet address calling the deposit function on the Escrow
Contract.

\subsection*{4.2 Funds Locked}

Upon confirmation of the deposit on chain, the Escrow Contract will reflect the Funded
state. Blockmediary will notify both Parties. The Seller may then proceed to ship goods
and prepare the Required Documents.

\subsection*{4.3 No Blockmediary Custody}

The Escrow Amount is held by the Escrow Contract, not by Blockmediary. Blockmediary does
not at any point hold, control, or have access to the Escrow Amount, except through the
releaser role functions, being the record verdict and refund functions, which it may only
call in accordance with this Agreement.

\subsection*{4.4 Know Your Customer, Sanctions, and Export Controls Gate}

The Buyer's deposit will be blocked unless each of the following conditions is satisfied
before funding.

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item Both Parties have completed know your customer and know your business verification
to Blockmediary's satisfaction.
\item Neither Party, nor any wallet address associated with either Party, appears on
applicable sanctions lists maintained by the Office of Foreign Assets Control, the United
Nations, His Majesty's Treasury, or the United Arab Emirates.
\item The goods described in Schedule 1 are not subject to an export licence requirement
under the Export Control Order 2008, the European Union's Dual Use Regulation 2021/821, or
the United States Export Administration Regulations or International Traffic in Arms
Regulations \citep{ukeco2008, eudualuse2021, usear, usitar}, or the Seller has confirmed
that it holds a valid applicable export licence and has provided a copy.
\item The trade corridor is not a sanctioned or prohibited corridor.
\end{enumerate}

Blockmediary will notify the Parties of any hold and its reason. A hold is not a
termination of the Deal; the Parties may resolve the underlying issue and request
re-screening.

\section*{5. Document Submission}
%---------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection*{5.1 Seller's Obligation}

The Seller shall submit the Required Documents via the Blockmediary platform on or before
the Submission Deadline specified in Schedule 1. The Seller warrants that all documents
submitted are genuine, have not been altered, and accurately reflect the underlying trade.

\subsection*{5.2 Completeness}

A presentation that omits any Required Document is an incomplete presentation and will not
be assessed for compliance. Blockmediary will notify the Seller of any missing document so
that it may be supplied before the Submission Deadline.

\subsection*{5.3 Document Format}

Documents must be submitted as high resolution scanned files of paper originals, at a
minimum resolution specified in Schedule 1. The Seller warrants that each scanned document
is a true and accurate copy of the original and that the original has not been separately
transferred or encumbered.

\subsection*{5.4 Shipment Deadline}

Documents must evidence shipment on or before the Shipment Deadline specified in Schedule
1. The shipment date is determined from the transport document, being the bill of lading
date, the air waybill issue date, or an equivalent, rather than from the Seller's
representations. A shipment date after the Shipment Deadline is a Discrepancy.

%---------------------------------------------------------------
\section*{6. Document Verification}
%---------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection*{6.1 Review Period}

Blockmediary shall complete its document review and issue either a Notice of Release or a
Discrepancy Notice within the review period specified in Schedule 1, defaulting to five
banking days, of receipt of a complete presentation. This mirrors the five banking day
standard set by UCP 600 Article 14(a) \citep{ucp600}. \textit{Open discussion point:
confirm or vary the default review period.}

\subsection*{6.2 Standard of Examination}

Blockmediary examines documents on their face against the Release Rules in the Escrow
Specification. Data in documents need not be identical across documents but must not
conflict, consistent with UCP 600 Article 14(b); the invoice goods description must match
the Escrow Specification exactly; amounts are compared in the token's minor units using
deterministic code rather than manual calculation; and quantity tolerances apply of plus
or minus five per cent on quantities not expressed in units, widening to plus or minus ten
per cent only where the Escrow Specification uses the qualifier approximately or about,
consistent with UCP 600 Article 30 \citep{ucp600}.

\subsection*{6.3 Compliant Presentation, Mandatory Release}

If Blockmediary determines that a presentation is Compliant, it shall issue a Notice of
Release. Upon expiry of the Objection Window with no valid Permitted Objection,
Blockmediary must call the record verdict function on the Escrow Contract, moving the Deal
to the Release Pending state. Once in that state, the release function on the Escrow
Contract is permissionless: any party, including the Seller directly, may call it to
transfer the Escrow Amount to the Seller's wallet.

\textit{Blockmediary has no discretion to delay or refuse release once a Compliant
determination has been made and the Objection Window has expired with no valid objection.
This is the core trust guarantee given to the Seller.}

\subsection*{6.4 Review Deadline Failure}

If Blockmediary fails to issue either a Notice of Release or a Discrepancy Notice within
the review period, Blockmediary is precluded from later claiming that the documents were
non compliant, consistent with the principle underlying UCP 600 Article 16 \citep{ucp600}.
In that event, Blockmediary shall treat the presentation as Compliant and proceed to issue
a Notice of Release. The Seller may contact Blockmediary support to trigger this remedy.

\subsection*{6.5 Human Reviewer Sign-Off}

For any Deal with an Escrow Amount above the value cap specified in Schedule 1, or where
artificial intelligence assisted extraction confidence falls below the threshold specified
in Schedule 1 on any checked field, a human document reviewer must sign off on the
compliance verdict before a Notice of Release is issued. \textit{Open discussion point:
confirm the default value cap.}

\section*{7. Objection Window and Release}
%---------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection*{7.1 Notice of Release}

Upon a Compliant determination, Blockmediary will notify both Parties. The Buyer's
Objection Window begins at the time of that notification.

\subsection*{7.2 Permitted Objections, Exhaustive List}

The Buyer may raise an objection during the Objection Window only on the following
grounds, referred to as Permitted Objections.

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item A Required Document is missing from the presentation.
\item A document field materially conflicts with the Escrow Specification, for example the
seller name, buyer name, amount, currency, or port of loading or discharge, in a way not
already identified by Blockmediary.
\item The transport document evidences shipment after the Shipment Deadline.
\item The Buyer has reasonable grounds, supported by evidence, to believe a submitted
document is fraudulent.
\item A sanctions or know your customer compliance issue has arisen after the Notice of
Release that was not known at the time of funding.
\item Both Parties mutually agree to request an amendment to the Release Rules, in which
case clause 8.3 applies.
\end{enumerate}

The following do not constitute Permitted Objections and will be rejected: a change of
mind about the trade; dissatisfaction with the goods, unless an inspection certificate is
itself a Release Rule and that certificate reports a deficiency; a wish to renegotiate
price or terms after shipment; any subjective quality complaint not evidenced by a
Required Document; and any ground not listed in (a) through (f) above. The Buyer's right
to object is strictly limited to these grounds. This limitation is fundamental to the
value the product provides to the Seller and is accepted by the Buyer at the time of
approving this Agreement.

\subsection*{7.3 Raising a Permitted Objection}

A Permitted Objection must be raised in writing via the Blockmediary platform during the
Objection Window, stating the specific ground relied upon from clause 7.2, the specific
document or documents and field or fields in issue, and, where clause 7.2(d) or 7.2(e)
applies, supporting evidence. Blockmediary will assess whether the objection falls within
clause 7.2. A determination that an objection is not a Permitted Objection will be
recorded in the Audit Ledger together with reasons.

\subsection*{7.4 No Valid Objection}

If the Objection Window expires with no Permitted Objection raised, or any objection
raised is determined to fall outside clause 7.2, Blockmediary shall proceed with the
record verdict function and the release function may then be called to transfer the
Escrow Amount to the Seller.

%---------------------------------------------------------------
\section*{8. Discrepancy, Amendment and Waiver}
%---------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection*{8.1 Discrepancy Notice}

If Blockmediary determines that the presentation is Discrepant, it will issue a
Discrepancy Notice to the Seller identifying each specific Discrepancy, within the review
period set out in clause 6.1. Blockmediary will not rely on any Discrepancy not stated in
the Discrepancy Notice.

\subsection*{8.2 Seller's Options on Discrepancy}

On receipt of a Discrepancy Notice, the Seller may proceed in one of three ways.

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item Cure: re-present corrected documents before the Submission Deadline. A
re-presentation restarts the review period under clause 6.1.
\item Request waiver: request that the Buyer waive the identified Discrepancy or
Discrepancies and agree to release. The Buyer's waiver must be given in writing via the
platform. A valid waiver authorises Blockmediary to treat the presentation as Compliant for
the waived Discrepancy or Discrepancies and to proceed to release.
\item Accept refund: where the Seller cannot cure and the Buyer will not waive, the Seller
may request a refund of the Escrow Amount to the Buyer, terminating the Deal.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{8.3 Amendment}

If both Parties wish to change the Release Rules or other material terms of this
Agreement, being a mutual amendment, the Deal must be terminated, the Escrow Amount
refunded to the Buyer, and a new Deal created with an updated Escrow Specification. This
follows from the smart contract architecture: the Escrow Specification hash committed on
chain cannot itself be altered without deploying a new deal.

%---------------------------------------------------------------
\section*{9. Refund}
%---------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection*{9.1 Refund Conditions}

Blockmediary shall call the refund function on the Escrow Contract and return the Escrow
Amount to the Buyer's wallet if any of the following conditions, referred to as Refund
Conditions, are met.

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item The Seller has not submitted a complete presentation by the Submission Deadline.
\item The Seller has submitted a Discrepant presentation that cannot be cured or waived,
and the Seller requests a refund under clause 8.2(c).
\item Both Parties have mutually agreed in writing to cancel the Deal.
\item A dispute has been resolved in the Buyer's favour by the Dispute Forum specified in
Schedule 2.
\item A sanctions or export control issue has been identified that prevents release under
applicable law.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{9.2 Refund Processing}

A refund requires human reviewer sign off within Blockmediary before the refund function
is called on chain. Refunds are typically processed within the period specified in
Schedule 1, defaulting to two banking days, of a Refund Condition being confirmed.
Blockmediary will notify both Parties.

\section*{10. Dispute Resolution}
%---------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection*{10.1 First Line: Blockmediary Determination}

Any dispute about whether a Permitted Objection is valid, whether a Discrepancy exists, or
whether a Refund Condition has been met shall first be determined by Blockmediary, acting
as the independent escrow operator. Blockmediary's determination is made on the face of
the documents against the Escrow Specification and is recorded in the Audit Ledger.

\subsection*{10.2 Escalation for Documentary Disputes: ICC DOCDEX}

If a Party disputes Blockmediary's determination on a documentary matter, being whether a
presentation is Compliant or Discrepant, either Party may refer the matter to the
International Chamber of Commerce's Documentary Instruments Dispute Resolution Expertise
procedure, known as DOCDEX \citep{iccdocdex}. A DOCDEX panel of three accredited experts
will deliver a non binding expert opinion within thirty days. The Parties agree to
consider the DOCDEX opinion in good faith before escalating further. DOCDEX is not
arbitration; its decision is binding only if both Parties agree in advance. It is fast and
lower cost than arbitration, which makes it well suited to documentary disputes
specifically.

\subsection*{10.3 Escalation for Other Disputes: Arbitration}

All other disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, including disputes
about fraud, breach of contract, or liability, shall, if not resolved by negotiation within
the period specified in Schedule 2, be finally resolved by arbitration under the rules
specified in Schedule 2, with the seat of arbitration and language of proceedings likewise
specified there. \textit{Open discussion point: confirm the forum. International Chamber
of Commerce arbitration is the global standard for trade disputes; London Court of
International Arbitration arbitration suits disputes seated in London specifically.}

\subsection*{10.4 Escrow Hold During Dispute}

When a Permitted Objection is raised and Blockmediary determines it is valid, or when a
dispute is escalated, Blockmediary will not call the record verdict function, keeping the
Deal in the Funded state. The Escrow Amount remains held on chain until the dispute is
resolved or a Refund Condition is met. The Audit Ledger records the hold and its reason.

\subsection*{10.5 Governing Law}

This Agreement, and any dispute arising from it, shall be governed by and construed in
accordance with the laws of England and Wales, unless the Parties have specified an
alternative governing law in Schedule 1.

\section*{11. Pause: Emergency Control}
%---------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection*{11.1 Permitted Grounds}

Blockmediary may invoke the pause function on the Escrow Contract, which suspends all fund
movements including the permissionless release function, only on the following grounds.

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item A critical vulnerability or exploit in the Escrow Contract has been identified or is
reasonably suspected.
\item A regulatory direction, court order, or competent authority instruction requires
suspension.
\item A sanctions or export control issue has been identified, after a Notice of Release
has been issued, that prevents release under applicable law.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{11.2 Prohibited Use}

Blockmediary must not invoke the pause function as a routine operational control, to delay
a compliant payout for administrative convenience, or for any reason not listed in clause
11.1. Use of the pause function outside these grounds, in circumstances where the Seller
is entitled to release, is a breach of this Agreement.

\subsection*{11.3 Notification and Resolution}

If the pause function is invoked, Blockmediary will notify both Parties immediately with
the stated reason and an estimated resolution timeline, and will lift the pause as soon as
the grounds are resolved. If the pause function is invoked on a Deal in the Release
Pending state without a valid ground under clause 11.1, Blockmediary shall be liable to
the Seller for any direct loss caused by the delay.

\item In the case of the Buyer only, the Buyer's wallet address is within the Buyer's sole
control and the Buyer has not authorised any third party to operate it for this Deal.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{13. Data Protection}
%---------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection*{13.1 Data Controller}

Blockmediary is the data controller for personal data processed in connection with this
Agreement, including know your customer and know your business records, party names, and
contact details. Processing is on the lawful basis of contract performance and, for
sanctions and anti money laundering screening, legal obligation, under Article 6 of the
General Data Protection Regulation \citep{gdpr2016}.

\subsection*{13.2 Off Chain Only}

All personal data, including party names, identification documents, contact details, and
wallet addresses linked to identity, is held in Blockmediary's off chain database and is
never written to the Blockchain. The Escrow Contract stores only the Buyer and Seller
wallet addresses, the Escrow Amount, the Deal state, and the Escrow Specification hash.
\textit{Open discussion point: if names are stripped from the Escrow Specification before
hashing, this clause should confirm explicitly that the Escrow Specification hash is
computed over a version of the specification containing no personal data.}

\subsection*{13.3 Retention}

Personal data will be retained for the period specified in Schedule 1, defaulting to five
years from the completion of the Deal, whether Released, Refunded, or Cancelled, to comply
with the record keeping obligations under regulation 40 of the Money Laundering
Regulations 2017 \citep{mlr2017}. After that period, data will be securely deleted or
anonymised.

\subsection*{13.4 Subject Rights}

Each Party has rights to access, rectify, and request erasure of their personal data,
subject to overriding legal obligations, in particular anti money laundering record
keeping. Requests should be directed to the data protection contact recorded in Schedule
1.

\subsection*{13.5 Cross Border Transfers}

If personal data is transferred outside the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area,
for example to a cloud provider or a know your business vendor in a third country,
Blockmediary will ensure that an appropriate transfer mechanism is in place, whether the
United Kingdom International Data Transfer Agreement, European Union standard contractual
clauses, an adequacy decision, or an equivalent mechanism.

\section*{14. Liability}
%---------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection*{14.1 Limitation}

Blockmediary's liability to either Party under or in connection with this Agreement is
limited to direct losses caused by Blockmediary's fraud, wilful misconduct, or gross
negligence, and in any event to the amount of fees paid by the claimant Party to
Blockmediary in connection with this Deal in the twelve months preceding the claim.

\subsection*{14.2 Exclusions}

Blockmediary shall not be liable for loss arising from the quality, condition, or non
delivery of goods, since Blockmediary releases funds on the basis of document compliance
rather than goods receipt; loss arising from a Party's own misrepresentation in documents
or in the know your customer process; loss arising from a smart contract exploit or a
force majeure event beyond Blockmediary's reasonable control, provided Blockmediary has
complied with its key management and audit obligations; or indirect or consequential loss,
loss of profit, or loss of business.

\subsection*{14.3 Force Majeure}

Neither Party shall be liable for delay or failure to perform caused by circumstances
beyond their reasonable control. If a force majeure event prevents the Seller from
shipping by the Shipment Deadline, the Parties may agree an extension by mutual amendment
under clause 8.3.

%---------------------------------------------------------------
\section*{15. Term and Termination}
%---------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection*{15.1 Term}

This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until the Deal reaches a
terminal state, being Released, Refunded, or Cancelled.

\subsection*{15.2 Cancellation Before Funding}

Either Party may cancel the Deal before the Buyer deposits the Escrow Amount. Cancellation
is effected by written notice via the Blockmediary platform, and Blockmediary will call
the cancel function on the Escrow Contract.

\subsection*{15.3 Survival}

Clauses 10, being Dispute Resolution, 12, being Representations and Warranties, 13, being
Data Protection, 14, being Liability, and this clause 15.4 survive termination or expiry
of this Agreement.

\subsection*{15.4 Governing Law, Survival}

The governing law clause at 10.5 and this survival clause survive any termination of this
Agreement.

\section*{16. General}
%---------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection*{16.1 Entire Agreement}

This Agreement, including all Schedules, constitutes the entire agreement between the
Parties relating to the escrow arrangement and supersedes all prior representations about
it. It does not affect or replace the Parties' underlying sale contract.

\subsection*{16.2 Amendments}

This Agreement may only be amended by mutual written agreement of all three parties
recorded in the Blockmediary platform. Amendments to the Release Rules require a new Deal
under clause 8.3.

\subsection*{16.3 No Assignment}

No Party may assign or transfer their rights or obligations under this Agreement without
the prior written consent of the other Parties, except that Blockmediary may assign its
rights and obligations to a group company or to a successor entity acquiring all or
substantially all of Blockmediary's business.

\subsection*{16.4 Severability}

If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining
provisions continue in full force.

\subsection*{16.5 Notices}

Notices under this Agreement shall be given via the Blockmediary platform's notification
system, or by email to the address registered by each Party. Notices are effective on
delivery confirmation.

\subsection*{16.6 No Third Party Rights}

\section*{Schedule 1: Deal Terms and Release Rules}

This Schedule is completed for each Deal and is incorporated into, and governed by, the

\subsection*{Deal Terms}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=3.2cm, style=nextline]
\item[Deal ID] The identifier matching the on chain deal identifier.
\item[Escrow Contract address] The address on the Blockchain at which the Escrow Contract
is deployed.
\item[Blockchain] Base Sepolia or Base Mainnet, with the corresponding chain identifier.
\item[Buyer legal name and wallet address] As provided by the Buyer at onboarding.
\item[Seller legal name and wallet address] As provided by the Seller at onboarding.
\item[Settlement Token] USDC or EURC.
\item[Escrow Amount] The amount in the Settlement Token, expressed in minor units.
\item[Invoice Currency] The currency stated in the commercial invoice, being United States
dollars, euro, or pounds sterling.
\item[Goods description and Harmonised System code] As provided at deal setup.
\item[Incoterm] The applicable Incoterms 2020 rule and named port or place.
\item[Trade corridor] The origin country and destination country.
\item[Funding Deadline] The date by which the Buyer must deposit the Escrow Amount.
\item[Shipment Deadline] The latest shipment date evidenced by the transport document.
\item[Submission Deadline] The latest date for the Seller's document presentation.
\item[Objection Window] Defaulting to forty eight hours after the Notice of Release.
\item[Value cap] Defaulting to fifty thousand pounds sterling equivalent; above this,
human reviewer sign off is required.
\item[Governing law] English law by default, or an alternative as agreed by the Parties.
\item[Dispute forum] As set out in Schedule 2.
\end{description}

\subsection*{Required Documents}

The Incoterm selected for this Deal drives the baseline set of Required Documents. A
commercial invoice is always required, and must be issued by the Seller, name the Buyer as
consignee, state the Invoice Currency, and match the goods description in this Schedule. A
packing list is always required, with quantities consistent with the invoice. An on board
bill of lading is required for free on board, cost and freight, and cost, insurance, and
freight sea shipments, and must carry an on board notation and date, name the vessel, and
be clean, with no damage clauses. An air waybill is required for air shipments, with its
date of issue serving as the shipment date. A road or rail transport document is required
for land shipments, and a multimodal transport document is required for combined mode
shipments. An export customs declaration is required for most Incoterms other than ex
works. An insurance certificate or policy is required for cost, insurance, and freight
terms, at a minimum of Institute Cargo Clauses C cover, and for carriage and insurance
paid terms, at a minimum of Institute Cargo Clauses A cover, in both cases at no less than
one hundred and ten per cent of the invoice value; cover notes are not accepted. A
certificate of origin is required where a preferential tariff, a letter of credit
condition, or a buyer requirement calls for one. An inspection certificate is required
where quality or quantity verification has been specified, issued by the named inspector
before shipment. An export licence is required where the goods fall on a controlled list.
Import customs clearance evidence is required for delivered duty paid terms only. Any
further document specific to this Deal is recorded separately in the deal setup record.

\subsection*{Release Rules}

The following conditions must all be satisfied for a Compliant determination: every
Required Document ticked above has been submitted; the invoice amount, converted to minor
units, matches the Escrow Amount within the tolerance recorded at deal setup; the Seller
name on the invoice matches the Seller named in this Agreement to a fuzzy match score of
at least 0.8; the Buyer name on the invoice matches the Buyer named in this Agreement to
the same threshold; the invoice currency field matches the Invoice Currency recorded
above; the shipment date evidenced by the transport document is on or before the Shipment
Deadline; the transport document is clean, meaning free of damage, short shipment, or
condition clauses; and, where insurance is a Required Document, the coverage is at least
one hundred and ten per cent of the invoice amount. Any deal specific rule beyond this
baseline set is recorded separately in the deal setup record.

\section*{Schedule 2: Dispute Forum}
%---------------------------------------------------------------

The first line forum for documentary disputes is the International Chamber of Commerce's
DOCDEX procedure, which delivers a non binding expert opinion within thirty days
\citep{iccdocdex}. All other disputes escalate to arbitration, either under the
International Chamber of Commerce Rules of Arbitration or the London Court of
International Arbitration Rules, in the version in force at the date of the dispute, with
the seat of arbitration in London, England, and the language of proceedings English. The
number of arbitrators defaults to one, reflecting that a single arbitrator is faster and
less costly for typical small and medium sized enterprise deal values, though three
arbitrators may be agreed for larger disputes. An emergency arbitrator is available under
both the International Chamber of Commerce and London Court of International Arbitration
Rules, which is relevant where injunctive relief is needed to prevent a fraudulent
release.
