## Break-even and funding

- **Break-even target:** Q3 2029 – Q1 2030 (Month 36–48 from operational launch)
- **Cumulative loss to break-even:** ~$1.3–1.7M
- **Total funding need:** **$1.8–2.2M** (covers losses to break-even + 6-month operating buffer)

---

## Key assumptions

- Customer acquisition (EOY): 20 (Y1) → 110 (Y2) → 350 (Y3)
- Deals per customer per year: 1.5–2.5 (Y1, ramp) → 3 (Y2) → 3.5 (Y3, repeats compound)
- Avg ticket size: $25K (Y1) → $35K (Y2) → $50K (Y3)
- Blended settlement-fee take rate: 0.7% per trade (range across tiers)
- Tier mix (Tier A eBL / Tier C paper): 30 / 70 (Y1) → 50 / 50 (Y2) → 60 / 40 (Y3)
- Headcount: 6 founders (Y1) → 8 (Y2) → 12 (Y3)
- Founder salaries: $20K Y1 (equity-heavy) → $45K Y2 → $55K Y3
- SaaS Stream B requires 1 partner in Y1, 2–3 in Y2, 4–5 in Y3
- Yield share Stream C depends on regulated yield infrastructure becoming available
