## Allocation of $2M over 36 months

| Category | % | $ | What it funds |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Engineering & product build** | 40% | **$800K** | CTO + engineering team salaries; smart contract escrow, deal intake UI, document review console, OCR/rules engine; cloud and on-chain infrastructure |
| **Operations, people & G&A** | 22% | **$440K** | CEO / CFO / COO / CCO / CRO salaries; HR, legal entity setup, finance and admin tooling |
| **Compliance, legal & regulatory** | 18% | **$360K** | KYB / KYC providers, sanctions screening, DIFC / ADGM regulatory counsel, document forensics partner retainer (Traydstream / Cleareye) |
| **GTM & customer acquisition** | 15% | **$300K** | Founder-led BD travel, Gulf chamber memberships, freight forwarder partnership development, trade events (Gulfood, GITEX, Dubai FinTech Summit), pilot incentives |
| **Working capital + contingency** | 5% | **$100K** | 6-month operating buffer; FX, on-chain gas reserves, unforeseen partner integration costs |
| **Total** | **100%** | **$2.0M** | |

---

## What this gets us

- **End of Year 1:** Operational platform on testnet → mainnet; 15–25 active customers; first SaaS partner integration
- **End of Year 2:** 75–150 active customers; 2–3 SaaS partners; sandbox graduation (DIFC or ADGM)
- **End of Year 3:** 250–450 active customers; $400K–$1M annual revenue; approaching operational break-even
