## 1. Beachhead — where we start

> **UAE importers of Indian and Türkish textiles, garments, and consumer goods.**
> $10K–$50K per shipment. 2–4 shipments per year. Repeat-purchase patterns.

**Why this corridor mix:**
- UAE–India: CEPA active since 2022; high trade volume; large Indian diaspora in UAE provides warm-intro pathway
- UAE–Türkiye: CEPA in force since September 2023; Türkish lira inflation drives SME stablecoin adoption — stablecoin-native validation built in
- Both corridors are textiles/garments-heavy — same product spec, same document set, same deal shape
- $10K–$50K = LC dead zone — too small for banks to serve economically, too large for trust-only payment
- Lower fraud profile than electronics; lower regulatory complexity than dual-use goods

---

## 2. GTM motion — how we win

**Primary — Founder-led BD via Gulf freight forwarders**
- Forwarders see SMEs hitting the trust-gap problem before banks do
- They have nothing to lose from referring us — they want the trade to close
- 1:1 founder conversations build customer-development data nothing else can replicate

**Secondary (parallel) — Regulator engagement as credibility infrastructure**
- DIFC FinTech Hive / ADGM RegLab applications during build period
- Not a customer channel — a trust amplifier for every BD conversation

---

## 3. Acquisition mechanism

- **First deal only:** 50% off settlement fee
- **Founder-led white-glove onboarding** for the first 10 customers
- **Standard pricing from deal #2 onward** — no discount escalator

---

## 4. Retention mechanism — why deal #2 happens on Transakt

- **KYB done once, reused across deals** — onboarding friction goes to zero from deal #2
- **Verified counterparty status accrues** — buyer/seller reputation portable across deals
- **Saved deal templates** — repeat trades with same counterparty load pre-filled
- **Tier A (eBL) speed advantage** compounds once both sides adopt electronic bills of lading

---

## Closing line

> *Narrow corridor, sharp wedge, founder-led learning loop.*
