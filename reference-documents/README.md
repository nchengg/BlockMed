# reference-documents

Real-world **sample** trade and company documents — examples of the document types Blockmediary's escrow handles. Used as references for building and testing the verification rules engine; they map to the `DOC-xx` catalogue in [../docs/document-templates.md](../docs/document-templates.md).

## Shipment / trade documents
- `commercial_invoice.pdf`, `Packing_List.pdf`
- `AWB_600a.txt`, `AWB_600b.pdf` (air waybills), `CMR_consignment_note.pdf` (road consignment)
- `Certificate_of_origin_UK.pdf`, `form_C1299_EUR1.pdf` (EUR.1 movement certificate)
- `Marine_Insurance_Certificate.pdf`, `SAD_Form.pdf` (customs declaration)
- `UK_Export_Health_Certificate.pdf`, `Halal_Certification.docx`, `ECAS_Example.jpg`

## Company / KYB documents
- `certificate_of_incorporation_UK.pdf`, `Certificate_of_Incorporation_UAE.jpeg`
- `AOA_UK.pdf` (articles of association)
- `PSC01.pdf`, `PSC02.pdf` (persons of significant control)

> All are public templates or synthetic samples — **no real customer data.**
